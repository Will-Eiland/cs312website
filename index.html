Purpose of the Website

This website's goal is to log and track Amiibo for collectors. It also will give relevant information for the people who want to search them up. I want an easy way to navigate what I own, do not own, and relitive information to each Amiibo. It should be capible of updates and ease of use when it comes to navigation and organization of information.

Future plan

I plan to make this website to be able to log and remember amiibo for its users(me). I plan on making a page that lists all available Amiibo, a page that lists all that you activly have marked as "owned", a search page, and decription pages. I also plan on adding more if I see that I may enjoy the use of them and their existance. Also an Admin page that includes being able to add new entries.

This is being created because I myself am a collecter of these items called "Amiibo". These are a Nintendo product that is a combo of a NFC chip and a plastic character model that is marketed to have interactability in Nintendo video games. I personally plan on making use of this website much further into the future after the end of this class so I want to ensure its usefullness and capibility for updates.
